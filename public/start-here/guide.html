360 Dealer Portal — one-page guide

Home Care Provider Services · Online ordering, customer quotes and dealer business tools

Open the portal

1Dealer Overview

Your home page: purchases this year and all time, recent orders, purchases by month and by manufacturer, and your favorites for quick reorder. Start here has the tour video and walkthroughs.

2Shop Products

Every manufacturer line on your account. Open a line or search any product name, model or part number. Filter by category, sort, and switch between grid and list. Prices shown are your own dealer prices.

3A product page

Photos, your price and every option. Add to cart, Print Brochure for your customer, or Add to a quote. Provider Reimbursement shows the published Medicare and Medicaid allowance for coded products — a reference; the biller confirms each code and the payer’s rules.

4Cart & checkout

The cart re-checks every price with HCPS before you order and shows freight by each manufacturer’s rule. Each manufacturer becomes its own order with your PO.

5Customer Quotes & brochures

For each line choose the customer’s price — manufacturer MSRP, your saved retail price, or a one-time price. Save, then Print / PDF or Email. Your dealer cost never appears on a customer document; your dealership is the seller.

6Orders & Tracking

Every order with its PO and status. Open one for its lines, shipments and carrier tracking once the manufacturer sends it. Print any order; reorder favorites.

7Resources & Support

Document Library (catalogs, manuals, warranty documents) · Training & In-Service (book a time or send a request) · Service, Parts & Warranty (parts, warranty, repair or return, with photos) · My Requests & Appointments (status of each).

8Find local referral sources

Clinicians near you who ordered a product code from Original Medicare, from CMS’s published data — 25 miles around your branch by default, nearest first. “Withheld by CMS” means fewer than 11, never zero.

Resources & Support, or Reports, Growth & Ideas

9Reports, Growth & Ideas

What you bought by manufacturer and period, with Excel and PDF downloads and growth ideas from your own figures.

10Storefront, Showroom & Social Ads

Build a public product website for your dealership (no checkout — customers send you requests), plan your showroom floor, and make social ads from products you may sell.

11My Account

Company details, locations, logo, users and your manufacturer accounts — account numbers on file, lines you may order, and account applications.

12Help & Training

A how-to for every tool, What’s New, and Start here: the four-minute tour and the walkthroughs “Find a product and make a customer quote” and “Find local referral sources”.

13Victoria, your assistant

Tap the blue globe at the bottom right and ask in your own words — typed or spoken. She finds products and opens their pages, gives your dealer price on the lines you carry, shows what Medicare and Medicaid allow for a coded product, checks an order and its tracking, starts a draft customer quote, drafts social ads and brochures, and takes you to reports, referral sources and training. She answers from your own account only and never places orders, sends emails or publishes anything on her own. Start here → Meet Victoria is a one-minute guide in her voice.

Tip: the search box at the top of every page finds products across all your lines by name, model or part number. Need help? Call HCPS toll-free at 888-626-3140 (main 937-626-5141) or email info@homecareproviderservices.us.
